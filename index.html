    How it works

    • Win probability for every ball, from a model trained on this data (Brier ).
    • Leverage = how much the next ball could move it. Average ball = 1. Pressure ball = 1.5 or more.
    • Value = win probability added ÷ leverage faced, in runs per 100 balls. Small samples are pulled to average.
    • Score = 0.65 pressure value + 0.20 lift over usual + 0.15 consistency, averaged over three cut-offs.
